The problem

Packs take hours, and one wrong bubble matters.

Most pharmacies still rebuild each pack by hand from the pharmacy system, check it against last month on paper, and phone patients to confirm changes. BubbleTrack brings that whole cycle into one place.

How it works

From report to sealed pack

  1. 1

    Import the report you already print

    Drop in a screenshot, PDF or spreadsheet from your pharmacy system. Every row is read and checked: names, strengths, NDCs, directions, duplicates. Nothing is saved until you review it.

    • ✓ Paste a screenshot straight from the clipboard
    • ✓ Column layout remembered per report
    • ✓ Test kits and supplies left out of the pack
  2. 2

    Fill one medication at a time

    The pack is drawn like the real card, with each pill’s shape, colour and imprint. Filling mode starts with an empty card and lights up only the bubbles for the medication in your hand.

    • ✓ Half tablets drawn as halves
    • ✓ Every-other-day and alternating doses
    • ✓ Print a pack sheet on US Letter
  3. 3

    Check it against the latest report

    Upload this month’s report and every difference with the pack comes back as a suggestion: a new strength, a timing change, a medication to add or remove. Apply what is right; leave the rest.

    • ✓ Strength changes need a pharmacist
    • ✓ Every change is logged with who made it
  4. 4

    The patient confirms from a link

    Press Send to patient and paste the message into the messaging app you already use. The patient opens the link, types their date of birth, checks every medication and taps Looks good, or asks for a change.

    • ✓No app, account or password
    • ✓One link per pack, open for 72 hours
    • ✓Requests and messages land in your alerts

Questions

What is a bubble pack? +

A card of sealed bubbles, one per day and time of day, holding the doses a patient takes then. Pharmacies fill them every week or month to help patients take the right medication at the right time.

Do I need to install anything? +

No. BubbleTrack runs in the browser on any computer or tablet, and patients open their pack link on any phone.

Do you sign a BAA? +

Yes. Every pharmacy accepts our Business Associate Agreement when it signs up, on every plan.

Is there a free trial? +
