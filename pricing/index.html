Pricing questions

What counts as an active patient? +

A patient whose pack or medication list was worked on in the last 60 days. Other patients keep their history and don’t count toward your plan.

What is an AI credit? +

Reading one page of a screenshot or PDF with AI uses one credit, and so does matching the columns of a spreadsheet (none when a saved template already matches). Unused credits don’t roll over.

Can I cancel? +

Yes, any time from Settings › Billing. Your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for, and you can export your data.

Do you sign a BAA? +

Yes. Every pharmacy accepts our Business Associate Agreement when it signs up, on every plan.

What happens after the free trial? +

The 14-day trial runs on Pro features with lower limits. Choose any plan to continue; without one the pharmacy becomes read-only and your data is kept for 90 days.
