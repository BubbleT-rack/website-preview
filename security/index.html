Security & HIPAA

HIPAA-ready, with a BAA for every pharmacy

Your patients’ medication data is health information. Here is how we protect it.

A BAA with every customer

Every pharmacy accepts our Business Associate Agreement when it signs up, on every plan.

HIPAA-eligible AWS services

BubbleTrack runs on HIPAA-eligible Amazon Web Services in the United States, covered by our Business Associate Addendum with AWS. Patient data stays in the US.

Encryption

All traffic uses TLS. The database, file storage and backups are encrypted at rest with AWS-managed keys.

Each pharmacy is isolated

Every record belongs to one pharmacy, and the database itself enforces it with row-level security, in addition to the checks in our code. We test isolation on every change.

Access controls

Roles for owners, admins, pharmacists and technicians; strong passwords checked against known breaches; screens that lock when idle; personal PINs on shared computers. Patients open one pack link with their date of birth, and see only that pack.

Audit logs

Changes to patient and medication data are logged with who made them and when. Our own support staff’s actions are logged separately, and they can’t open patient records.

Backups

Automatic daily database backups kept for 14 days, with point-in-time recovery.

Your data, your choice

Export everything at any time. When a pharmacy leaves, its data is deleted after the retention period in our terms.

Found a security issue?

Please email hello@bubbletrack.app. We reply within two business days and don’t take action against good-faith research.
